Orlando International Airport (MCO) posts live wait times for its three TSA checkpoints: Gates 1-59, Gates 70-129 and Gates C230-C254. This page shows those posted times for the TSA Standard and TSA PreCheck lanes exactly as Orlando International posts them, with the time of each reading. The checkpoints close overnight and reopen early in the morning, so the rows read Closed in the small hours. Terminal C (Gates C230-C254) is a separate building reached by the Terminal Link train before security, so pick the checkpoint for your gates. The numbers come from Orlando International Airport; AirportScout adds nothing to them.
